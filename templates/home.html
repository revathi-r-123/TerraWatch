How TerraWatch works

TerraWatch is a shared environmental reporting platform for panchayats and municipalities. It connects residents with the local teams responsible for reviewing, responding to, and resolving community issues.

For local government

Panchayat or municipality administrators can turn community reports into organised action, monitor progress, coordinate field work, and maintain a clear record of environmental service delivery.

Who does what?

  • Citizens: report local environmental problems and follow their progress.
  • Authorities: verify reports, plan inspections, assign work, and track resolution.
  • HKS workers: carry out assigned maintenance work, record attendance, and update availability.
